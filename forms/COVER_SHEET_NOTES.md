**Obtain the official form** from the current USPTO website (search “PTO/SB/16” or access via Patent Center). Do not rely on outdated copies.

## Micro-Entity Certification

If claiming micro-entity status you must certify eligibility (income threshold and prior-application limits). False certification can have serious consequences. Confirm current requirements on uspto.gov.

## Filing Fee

Verify the current provisional application fee for your entity status on the USPTO fee schedule. The micro-entity rate cited in educational materials was $65 as of the 2026 schedule; fees change periodically.

## Electronic Filing

Self-represented (pro se) applicants may file through Patent Center. Create a USPTO.gov account well before the intended filing date.
